**Decisions.** The maintainers review and merge contributions, decide what goes into a release, and keep the
evidence rules (evidence classes are never mixed or averaged; every value names its source and state). Changes to
those rules, to the licences or to this document need the agreement of the lead maintainer, after discussion in a
public issue where possible.

**Accepting data.** A correction or a new value is accepted only after a maintainer has checked it against its
page or dataset (contributions/SCHEMA.md, Acceptance procedure). A maintainer does not accept their own data
contribution; another maintainer, or an independent contributor, checks it.

**Releases.** Each release is versioned and archived on Zenodo with DOIs. Accepted corrections take effect in the
next release; the old value stays in the record's history with the contributor's credit.

**Credit.** Contributors are listed in `CONTRIBUTORS.md` and, if they opt in, in the Zenodo metadata of the
release. Individual values carry their contributors.

**Conduct.** Everyone in ORCHID spaces follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md); reports go to
conduct@orchid-project.org and are handled by the maintainers in confidence.

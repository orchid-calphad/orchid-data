10 phases (tier A 8, tier C 1, liquid 1); 7 stable in a calculated diagram. Structure types are assigned only when certain (tier A: standard phase name; tier B: unique Materials Project + OQMD on-hull match, DFT-supported; tier C: one structure stated by the assessment's crystallographic table, assessor-stated); other phases list candidates and are marked needs_review.

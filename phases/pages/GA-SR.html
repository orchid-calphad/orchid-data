Ga–Sr phases

8 phases (tier A 6, tier B 1, liquid 1); 7 stable in a calculated diagram. Structure types are assigned only when certain (tier A: standard phase name; tier B: unique Materials Project + OQMD on-hull match, DFT-supported; tier C: one structure stated by the assessment's crystallographic table, assessor-stated); other phases list candidates and are marked needs_review. Stability is from phase diagrams calculated with pycalphad from the SGTE binary collection (evidence class: model).
